State the –emia

— pH —
–emia describes the patient's current blood pH. –osis describes a process acting on it — a patient's state can be acidemia, alkalemia, or normal, while one or more –osis processes are still underway.
Normal pH does not exclude major acid–base abnormality — see Process below.
